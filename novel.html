Component 3

The Map of the World

Three key locations where the defining events of Rich Dad Poor Dad unfold — each with a Travel Guide entry capturing why it matters to the story.

Hawaii → Corporate America — Key Story Locations

Location 01

Poor Dad’s Home

Hilo, Hawaii — Chapter 1

This modest Hawaii home is where Robert grew up watching his educated father struggle with bills despite a government salary. It was here Robert first noticed that a high income doesn’t guarantee financial security. Chapter 1, p. 4

Location 02

Rich Dad’s Office & Store

Honolulu, Hawaii — Chapter 2

Rich Dad ran his empire from a small office above his store, where he taught Robert and Mike real-world money lessons instead of textbook theories. Major decisions about Robert’s financial education were made in this room. Chapter 2, p. 22

Location 03 — Conceptual

The Rat Race

Corporate America — Chapter 4

The “Rat Race” represents the cycle millions follow: work hard, get paid, pay bills, repeat. Robert’s journey is defined by his determination to escape this trap and build true financial freedom. Chapter 4, p. 55
Component 4

One Word That Defines the Entire Book

MINDSET

Every lesson in this book begins and ends with how you think. Rich Dad didn’t teach Robert to work harder — he taught him to think differently. The rich see opportunities where the poor see obstacles. Without changing your mindset, no amount of money can set you free.

“It is not the smart who get ahead, but the bold.”

— Rich Dad Poor Dad, p. 154
